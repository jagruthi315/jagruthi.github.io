About

Comfortable in the mess before the dashboard

I'm a data analyst who likes the unglamorous part of the job — cleaning a messy CSV, tracing a broken join, deciding which 3 numbers on a 40-column sheet actually matter. Power BI, Tableau, SQL, Excel and Python are the tools; the goal is always the same: turn a pile of rows into a decision someone can act on.

Alongside my B.Tech in Data Science, I co-founded CoLab Nation, where I run communication, team management and day-to-day workflows as COO — which is really just data-analyst thinking applied to people instead of pivot tables.
